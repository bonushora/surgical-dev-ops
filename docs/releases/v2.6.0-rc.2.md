Português: [v2.6.0-rc.2_PT-BR.md](./v2.6.0-rc.2_PT-BR.md)

Status: **qualified release candidate**

This release candidate promotes Reconstruction v3 and publishes a bilingual,
falsifiable deep adversarial review package.

## What changed

- R1 establishes canonical immutable operation-state contracts.
- R2 binds verified identity, separate human approval and exact capability authority.
- R3 binds physical workspace identity, qualified provider results and Manifest CAS.
- Production success requires canonical workspace/CAS evidence.
- Finalized replay rejects null, mutable, incomplete or substituted persisted bindings.
- Legitimate historical finalized replay remains compatible.

## Adversarial review

- Adjacent-only and black-box-only testing is explicitly insufficient.
- Direct white-box attacks against the deterministic core are mandatory.
- The playbook includes progressive campaign levels and safe directed demonstrations.
- Reproducible counterexamples must turn the affected qualification red.
- The challenge, playbook and report form are available in English and Portuguese.

Start here: [adversarial challenge](../review/TRY_TO_BREAK_IT.md).
