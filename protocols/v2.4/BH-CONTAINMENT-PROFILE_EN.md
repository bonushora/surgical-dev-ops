# Integrated containment operational profile — English

Active profile: `BH-SEP-v2.4+BH-SDP-v2.4+BH-CONTAINMENT-integrated-v1`.

Required normative components:
- [BH-SEP v2.4 — normative RAW](./BH-SEP.md)
- [BH-SDP v2.4 — normative RAW](./BH-SDP.md)

Containment in this version is integrated directly into the v2.4 normative RAW.
The legacy v2.3 RAW and the v2.3 BH-CONTAINMENT extension remain preserved as historical artifacts and cannot be used as a less restrictive fallback.

This document identifies the operational composition; it creates no authority, restores no previous authorization, and does not replace cryptographic validation by the trusted loader before protected actions.
