# Financial / Regulated deployment

The pilot is customer-controlled: on-premises, private cloud, private VPC, or an
isolated customer environment. Public multi-tenant SaaS is not required.
External telemetry is off, unnecessary egress is deny-by-default, production
dispatch is off until explicitly eligible, and physical authority stays local.

Enterprise identity, qualified segregation of duties, KMS/HSM integration,
institutional operating controls, independent assurance, and regulatory review
remain required before bank production use. This beta is not certification or a
compliance claim.
