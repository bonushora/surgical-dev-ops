# External Review Acknowledgements

## Boris Abuzov

Surgical DevOps acknowledges the independent external security and architecture
review contribution of **Boris Abuzov**.

At the current agreed depth of review, his contribution focused particularly on:

- delegated authority and authority lifetime;
- effective runtime authority;
- provenance and crash-recovery behavior;
- containment boundaries;
- identity continuity;
- enforcement placement;
- confused-deputy risks; and
- possible paths through which authority could be created, amplified,
  transferred or unintentionally preserved.

Questions raised during the review led to concrete hardening and additional
qualification work in Surgical DevOps, including work around authority lifetime,
provenance, crash recovery and effective runtime authority.

The current orchestrator review is considered **substantially complete at the
agreed depth of review**, with two previously identified qualification points
remaining. Going materially beyond this scope would constitute a broader
investigation rather than continuation of the same review.
