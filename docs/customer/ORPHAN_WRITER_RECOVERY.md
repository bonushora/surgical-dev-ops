# Orphan-writer recovery

Recovery requires an exact owner record and generation, correlated Surgical
journal evidence, absence of a competing valid writer, an exclusive recovery
claim, explicit local one-shot human recovery authority, and a final owner/state
CAS. Recovery publishes a durable result.

Elapsed time, missing PID, process absence, or blind unlink never proves an
orphan. A stale recovery actor cannot delete a replacement owner.
